Before you use this

This is a model, not a chart and not advice. The weights in it are estimates that have never been fitted against real dives. It can be confidently wrong about visibility, about conditions and about where fish are.

It does not know the law. Minimum sizes and closed seasons exist for only 5 of the 164 jurisdictions it covers. Everywhere else it shows no legal information at all, and that means unknown, never permitted.

It does not reliably know where you may not fish. Protected areas come from OpenStreetMap and its marine coverage is incomplete. A spot shown inside a reserve is entirely possible.

Breath-hold diving kills experienced people. Shallow water blackout gives no warning and drowns silently. Never dive alone, never hyperventilate, and always dive with someone watching you on the surface.

You are responsible for your own safety and for knowing the rules where you dive. Verify everything with the local authority before you get in the water.

    Data sources and licences

    Everything here is built from free, public data. Where a licence requires attribution, this is it.

    • OpenStreetMap contributors, under ODbL — base map, and the coastline, wrecks and protected areas the model derives its land mask and exclusions from, via Overpass.
    • OpenSeaMap — seamark overlay, ODbL.
    • EMODnet Bathymetry — European seas, ~90 m. emodnet.ec.europa.eu
    • GMRT (Global Multi-Resolution Topography) and SRTM15+ via NOAA ERDDAP — bathymetry outside Europe.
    • Open-Meteo — marine, weather and river discharge forecasts. open-meteo.com, CC BY 4.0.
    • Copernicus Marine Service — temperature at depth, where credentials are configured.
    • Copernicus Sentinel-2 — satellite water clarity, where configured.

    Protected areas are from OpenStreetMap, not the WDPA. Marine coverage is incomplete. The absence of a mask is not evidence that there is no reserve.

    Usage counting. If a counting endpoint is configured, this page reports one short request per view: a page path, the title and the referring site. No cookies, no identifiers, no tracking between visits, and never any coordinates — a point forecast is reported as /point, never as the latitude and longitude in the address bar. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are honoured. Nothing is sent if no endpoint is configured.

    This is model output. It is not a nautical chart, not a tide table and not legal advice. Verify everything locally before you dive.
